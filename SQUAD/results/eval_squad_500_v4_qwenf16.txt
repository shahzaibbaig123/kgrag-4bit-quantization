Photovoltaics is a direct method of converting sunlight into electricity. It works by using the photoelectric effect, where sunlight is absorbed by photovoltaic cells, typically made of silicon, which then generates an electric current. **Concentrated Solar Power (CSP):**
Concentrated Solar Power is an indirect method that converts sunlight into electricity. In CSP systems, sunlight is focused using mirrors or lenses to a small area, where it is used to heat a fluid. This fluid then drives a turbine connected to a generator, which produces electricity (Reference [2]). Tracking systems are essential in CSP systems to ensure that the mirrors or lenses follow the sun's movement, maximizing the amount of sunlight captured (Reference [3]).
