Answer: The oldest known fossil among the Eutheria group is Juramaia sinensis, or "Jurassic mother from China," which dates back to 160 million years ago in the Late Jurassic period. This small shrewlike fossil is considered the oldest known eutherian mammal, marking a significant point in the evolutionary lineage that includes placental mammals.
